2. Ahrefs — Best for Backlink Analysis

If you're serious about link building, you need Ahrefs. Their backlink database is the largest in the industry — over 40 trillion links indexed.

Why we use it: Site Explorer shows you exactly who links to your competitors and how to get those same links. The Content Gap feature finds keywords your competitors rank for that you don't.

Pricing: Starts at $129/mo (7-day trial for $7)

3. Surfer SEO — Best Content Optimization Tool

Surfer SEO analyzes the top-ranking pages for any keyword and gives you a data-driven content brief. It tells you exactly what to include, how many words to write, and which terms to use.

Why we use it: Content Editor scores your draft in real-time against the top 20 results. You can optimize before publishing instead of after.

Pricing: Starts at $89/mo

4. Screaming Frog SEO Spider — Best Technical SEO Tool

Free for up to 500 URLs. Crawls your entire site and finds broken links, redirects, duplicate content, missing meta tags, and hundreds of other technical issues.

Why we use it: The paid version handles unlimited URLs and includes JavaScript rendering. Run it before every site launch.

Pricing: Free (up to 500 URLs), £149/yr (unlimited)

→ Download Screaming Frog

5. Google Search Console — Best Free SEO Tool

It's free, it's from Google, and it tells you exactly how Google sees your site. Check indexing status, search queries, click-through rates, and core web vitals.

Why we use it: Performance report shows which queries drive traffic and what your average position is. Use it to find quick wins — pages that rank #8-12 that you can push into the top 5.

Pricing: Free

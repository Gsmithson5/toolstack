Phase 2: Analyzing Search Intent

In 2026, Google is obsessed with intent. There are four main types of intent:

  • Informational: Users want answers (e.g., "how to do keyword research").
  • Navigational: Users want a specific site (e.g., "Ahrefs login").
  • Commercial: Users are researching products (e.g., "best keyword research tools").
  • Transactional: Users are ready to buy (e.g., "buy Semrush subscription").

Your content must match the intent. If you write a sales page for an informational keyword, you will not rank.

Phase 4: Identifying "Quick Win" Long-Tail Keywords

Long-tail keywords are longer, more specific phrases. They often have lower search volume but much higher conversion rates because they are highly targeted. Use tools like AnswerThePublic to uncover what questions people are asking around your core topics.

Final Checklist for Your Keyword Selection

  • Is the monthly search volume sustainable for your traffic goals?
  • Is the Keyword Difficulty (KD) score realistic for your current domain?
  • Does the search intent align with the content you plan to create?
  • Are there current top-ranking pages that you can realistically outperform with better, more updated content?

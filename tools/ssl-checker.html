How to Check SSL Manually

  1. SSL Labs: Visit SSL Labs — the industry standard. Enter your domain for a full report including certificate chain, cipher strength, and vulnerabilities.
  2. Browser check: Click the padlock icon in your browser's address bar. It shows certificate issuer, expiry date, and connection security.
  3. OpenSSL command: Run openssl s_client -connect example.com:443 -servername example.com 2>/dev/null | openssl x509 -noout -dates
  4. What's My SSL: WhatsMySSL.com checks your browser's TLS support.
